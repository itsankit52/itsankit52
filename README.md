<h3>🔷 AcadIn – Academia-Industry Collaboration Platform</h3>

<p>
A platform designed to connect students, academic institutions, and companies by bridging the gap between education and industry. It enables students to explore internship and job opportunities, apply for positions, and connect with companies, while organizations can manage opportunities and discover talented candidates through an intuitive platform.
</p>

**✨ Key Features**

- Student and company profiles, internship and job postings, application management, and seamless interaction between academia and industry.
- AI-powered student assessments to evaluate skills and identify knowledge gaps.
- Personalized learning and skill recommendations.
- AI-driven job and internship recommendations based on students' skills, interests, and assessment results.

**💻 Tech Stack**

`React.js` `Tailwind CSS` `TypeScript` `Node.js` `Express.js` `MongoDB` `Mongoose` `JWT` `Bcrypt` `Cloudinary` `Google Gemini API` `REST API`

<p>
  🌐 <b>Live:</b> <a href="https://aip-qq08.onrender.com">AcadIn Platform</a>
</p>
